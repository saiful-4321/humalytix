# Employee Self-Service (ESS) Portal User Guide

## Overview
The ESS Portal allows employees to manage their own data, requests, and views without contacting HR for every detail.

## Accessing the Portal
1. Log in to the application.
2. In the Sidebar, locate the **Self Service** menu.
3. Click on **ESS Dashboard** or any specific feature.

## Features

### 1. ESS Dashboard
- **Overview**: View your Leave Balance, Today's Attendance status, and Last Month's Salary at a glance.
- **Quick Actions**: One-click access to Apply Leave, Regularize Attendance, View Payslips, etc.

### 2. My Profile
- **View**: See your personal and employment details.
- **Update**: Click "Edit Info" to update your Phone, Mobile, Address, Emergency Contact, and Profile Photo.

### 3. Attendance & Regularization
- **Attendance Log**: View your daily Check-in/Check-out times and status.
- **Regularization**: If you missed a punch or have a correction:
  1. Click the **Request Regularization** button.
  2. Select the Date and enter valid times.
  3. Provide a Reason.
  4. Submit. The status will be shown in the "Regularization Requests" table (Pending/Approved/Rejected).

### 4. My Leaves
- Takes you to the Leave Management page where you can Apply for Leave and view your leave history.

### 5. My Payslips
- **View**: See a list of your monthly salary details (Basic, Gross, Deductions, Net).
- **Download**: Click the **PDF** button to download your official payslip.

### 6. My Assets
- View a list of company assets (Laptops, Phones, etc.) assigned to you, including their condition and assignment date.

### 7. Performance Goals
- Takes you to the Performance Management section to track your assigned goals and update progress.

### 8. Holiday Calendar
- View a list of upcoming public holidays and company holidays.

### 9. Salary Certificate Requests
- **Request**: Need a formal letter (Salary Cert, NOC, Experience Letter)?
  1. Click **New Request**.
  2. Select the Letter Type.
  3. State the Reason (e.g., "For Bank Loan").
  4. Submit.
- **Download**: Once approved by HR, a **Download** button will appear next to your request.
